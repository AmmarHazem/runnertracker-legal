Support

How can we help?

For help with RunnerTracker, report a problem through the project’s GitHub issue tracker.

Contact support on GitHub

GitHub Issues are public. Do not include your route, exact location, health information, email address, or other personal information in a support request.

For private support or privacy questions, email ammar.hazem0@gmail.com.

Helpful details to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Whether location permission was Precise or Approximate.
  • Whether the issue occurred while the app was in the background or the screen was locked.

Privacy requests

RunnerTracker has no account or developer-operated server storage. You can delete a saved activity directly in the app; deleting the app also removes its local data from that device, subject to your iOS backup settings.
