Privacy Policy

Your run data stays on your device.

Last updated: July 25, 2026

What RunnerTracker uses

When you start a run, RunnerTracker uses your device location to record the route, distance, pace, elapsed time, movement estimates, and optional manual laps. It may use background location during an active run so tracking can continue while the app is not visible.

Where your data goes

RunnerTracker stores run information locally on your iPhone. We do not operate a backend, create user accounts, collect analytics, sell data, or receive your route or activity information.

Maps are provided with Apple system services. Apple may process map-tile or geocoding requests under its own privacy policies. RunnerTracker does not upload your recorded route to us.

Your choices

  • You choose when tracking starts and ends.
  • You can deny, revoke, or change location permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete any saved run in the app. Deleting a run removes its route, laps, and saved metrics from the device.
  • You can delete the app to remove its locally stored data from that device, subject to iOS backup settings you control.

Children

RunnerTracker is not directed to children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health and safety

Movement labels and fitness metrics are estimates, not medical information or medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the RunnerTracker support page. Do not include route information or other personal information in a public support request.
